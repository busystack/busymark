## Providers and recorded model inputs

The two Gemini requests used `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` via v1beta
Interactions. The two OpenAI requests used `gpt-realtime-2.1-mini` via the
Realtime WebSocket. All outputs were mono 24 kHz PCM WAV after composition.
English and French samples contained names, quoted imperative text and numeric
content. The matching OpenAI transcript and local waveform review were
recorded separately. Local recognition of one French imperative produced an
acoustically identical homophone. There were four requests eligible for billing, an
aggregate conservative reservation of US$0.40 and a paid-tier estimate of
US$0.009400 from reported usage. No invoice was observed.
